Momentum is a project tracker for open-ended project work;
the kind that's never really "done."

Rather than tracking completion, Momentum tracks engagement: 
how recently you touched a project, and how much time you've invested in it. 
There are no due dates, no priority scores, and no completion percentages.
It measures effort, not results.

Additionally, you can track important project ideas in the "Ideas" section
of a project.

Features:

* Track projects as active, planned, or archived
* Log sessions — what you did and how long you spent on it — to build a 
running record of your work
* Keep a running list of ideas per project, separate from your session log
* See at a glance which projects are getting stale, 
colour-coded by how recently you last worked on them

All data is stored locally in a SQLite database. 
No account, no login, no internet connection required, and 
nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
